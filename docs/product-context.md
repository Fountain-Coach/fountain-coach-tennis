# Product context

## Vinegarium

`vinegarium.de` is the customer-owned domain and business identity for this collaboration. The domain owner is also
one of the tennis players. Together, we are developing the business incrementally, starting with a focused spare-time
project rather than a broad platform build.

The first product is a tennis match and shared tennis round. In that sense, the Tennis application is the first small
step into the Vinegarium business: concrete, useful, and bounded enough to develop and operate carefully.

## Naming and domain boundary

- **Business/host identity:** `vinegarium`
- **Application:** Fountain Coach Tennisrunde
- **Publishing domain:** `tennis.fountain.coach`
- **Production host:** Hetzner server `vinegarium`

The publishing domain remains `tennis.fountain.coach` for customer-facing continuity. `vinegarium` names the
customer’s business context and infrastructure, not a second public application domain.

This public repository keeps the explanation privacy-safe: it does not identify the domain owner by name and does not
contain participant names, match history, or private business information.
